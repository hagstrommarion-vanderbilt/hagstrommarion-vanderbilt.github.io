Affordable microprocessor knee (AMPK)

Doctoral workProsthetic designHydraulicsAccessibility

Problem. Microprocessor-controlled knees offer useful functions, but cost and physical fit limit who can get one.

Approach. A compact knee that keeps the essential microprocessor-controlled behavior. Design drivers: affordability, build height, and consistent behavior.

Work. Hydraulic-system design, fabrication, controller development, and benchtop validation. Human-subject evaluation is part of the development path.

Backward-walking control

Semi-powered prosthesesSwing assistanceGait biomechanics

Problem. Backward stepping is part of everyday movement and poses distinct challenges for prosthetic-knee control.

Approach. An activity-specific controller that adds swing assistance to a swing-assist knee during backward walking.

Measures. Knee motion, foot clearance, and compensatory hip movement.

Headbands for pediatric hearing assessment

FabricationPediatric audiologyOngoing

Problem. During pediatric bone-conduction hearing assessments, headbands slip or fall off and the bone conductor moves.

My part. Fabricating headbands that hold the bone conductor in place. Collaboration with a Vanderbilt Doctor of Audiology (AuD) capstone project.
