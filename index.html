MECHANICAL ENGINEERING · VANDERBILT UNIVERSITY

Marion Hagstrom

Engineering prosthetics.
Expanding possibilities for mobility.

I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at Vanderbilt University, working with Dr. Michael Goldfarb in the CREATE / Goldfarb Lab. My research brings together prosthetic design, wearable-device control, and human biomechanics to make advanced lower-limb prostheses more accessible.

I develop affordable microprocessor-controlled prosthetic knees and control strategies for semi-powered knees, including backward walking and cycling. My work spans hydraulic design, prototyping, benchtop testing, and human-subject evaluation.

Before Vanderbilt, I earned my B.S. in Biomedical Engineering, magna cum laude, at the University of Florida. My experience in a prosthetics clinic continues to shape how I think about the connection between engineering design and everyday mobility.
